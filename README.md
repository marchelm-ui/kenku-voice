# Kenku Voice

An Android app for playing a kenku in D&D. Type a line and press Enter: every
2–3 words are spoken by a different stolen voice — new accent, pitch and speed —
and the whole line is saved as one audio file you can replay or share.

Works offline with the text-to-speech voices already on your phone. No account
or paid service needed.

## Install on your phone

1. On your Android phone, open this repository's **Releases** page.
2. Download **KenkuVoice.apk** from the newest release.
3. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.

Every push to `main` builds a new release automatically. Newer builds install
over older ones and keep your saved lines.

## More voices

Tap **More voices** in the app to open Android's text-to-speech settings.
Installing extra languages there (for example French, German, Hindi, Japanese)
adds more accents to the kenku's collection.
